FAQ

What people ask me

I'm not good with tech. Is that a problem?

Not at all. You send me your photos and prices, and I handle the rest. With the Care plan, one message is enough to change a price or your opening hours.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain name is in your name, and the website is yours once it's fully paid.

I already use Fresha, Booksy or another booking tool.

No problem: I connect your website to the tool you already use. Otherwise, I set up a free one for you.

What about hosting and Google SEO?

Your website is hosted on a fast, reliable service that is free for a showcase site. The domain name is in your name and you pay for it directly (about €10 to €15 a year), so you stay in control of your website. For Google, I set up the basics: titles and descriptions for each page, copy that uses the words your clients search for, and a fast, mobile-friendly site. I also set up your Google Business Profile, which matters most to be found in your area. I use artificial intelligence tools to work fast, but I check and fine-tune everything myself.

How much does the website cost each month?

Nothing is mandatory. The €29 monthly Care plan covers maintenance, updates and small changes. You can also manage everything yourself.

Do you work with clients outside France?

Yes, fully remotely. I build websites in any language, for clients in France and abroad. Sheyna's website in Tel Aviv, for example, is in French, English and Hebrew. Calls can be in English, French or Hebrew.
